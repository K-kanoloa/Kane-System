# Community conduct

Kane System contributors and maintainers are expected to keep technical
discussion respectful, specific and evidence-based.

- Treat other participants with patience and professional respect.
- Critique code, tests and decisions without attacking people.
- Do not publish private identities, credentials, logs or security details.
- Do not misrepresent test evidence, compatibility or project completion.
- Respect licensing and third-party attribution requirements.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption and disclosure of
private information are unacceptable. Report security matters through
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Other conduct concerns may be raised privately
with the repository Owner through an appropriate GitHub account channel.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when
necessary to protect the project and its community.
